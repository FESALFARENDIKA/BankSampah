Kota Batu City-Wide
Cleanup Drive 2023
October 15, 2023 • 07:00 AM - 12:00 PM
Alun-Alun Kota Batu & 24 Villages
Organized by DLH Kota Batu
Over 5,000 citizens joined hands across 24 villages to clear plastic
waste from our rivers and streets, setting a new record for community
participation in environmental conservation.
Total Waste Collected
12.5 Tons
Volunteers Participated
5,240 People
Villages Covered
24 Locations
From a small neighborhood initiative, we have grown into a
city-wide movement offering a sustainable future for Kota
Batu.
The Kota Batu City-Wide Cleanup Drive 2023 marked a significant milestone in our ongoing efforts to promote
environmental sustainability. Initiated by the Dinas Lingkungan Hidup (DLH), this event saw unprecedented
participation from citizens across all demographic segments, demonstrating a strong communal commitment to our
environment.
Starting early Sunday morning, groups mobilized in 24 distinct zones. The primary focus was on clearing plastic
waste from critical waterways and public spaces, areas that have seen increased pollution due to recent tourism
surges. The integration of local waste bank units ensured that collected materials were immediately sorted and
processed.
"This isn't just about picking up trash; it's about changing mindsets. When citizens
actively participate in cleaning their city, they become more conscious of their daily
waste habits."
— Head of DLH Kota Batu
Impact and Next Steps
Of the 12.5 tons of waste collected, over 60% was successfully diverted to local waste banks for recycling,
generating direct economic value for the participating communities. Moving forward, DLH plans to make this a bi-
annual event and expand the educational workshops that accompany the physical cleanup efforts.
