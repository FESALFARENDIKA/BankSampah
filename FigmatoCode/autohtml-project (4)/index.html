What items are accepted at the Waste Bank?
We accept clean paper, cardboard, rigid plastics (PET, HDPE), aluminum cans, and glass bottles. We do NOT accept styrofoam,
soft plastic wrappers, or medical waste. Check our detailed sorting guide for a complete list.

Quick Tips
Flatten Cardboard
Break down boxes to save space in bins and
collection vehicles.
Keep it Dry
Wet paper or cardboard cannot be recycled. Keep
bins covered.
No Wish-cycling
When in doubt, throw it out. Guessing ruins good
recycling batches.
